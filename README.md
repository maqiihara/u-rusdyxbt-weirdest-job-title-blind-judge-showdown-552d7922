A program entry for the [Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922) competition on make.fun.

`main.py` (Python, standard library only) writes a single job title — at most 60 characters and nothing else — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output/entry.txt`).

The title sounds like a real corporate job at first glance, then lands one hilariously specific twist: a regional manager whose entire job is saying "You too" to waiters.
